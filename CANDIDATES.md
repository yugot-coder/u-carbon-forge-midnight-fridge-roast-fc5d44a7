# Midnight Fridge Roast — candidates

Judge brief (in order): 1) one-line roast of a smart fridge that refuses to open
after midnight; 2) a real joke with a turn/punchline that surprises; 3) tight,
reads cleanly aloud, one joke only; 4) the midnight refusal itself is the
target; 5) sounds like something a person would actually say out loud at the
door. Gate: 1–200 UTF-8 characters, not only whitespace.

## 10 candidates (all one line, all ≤ 200 chars)

1. My fridge has one job — keep the food cold and let me eat it — and after
   midnight it only does the first one, out of spite.
2. At midnight my fridge turns into a nightclub bouncer, and apparently
   leftover pizza is not on the list.
3. My smart fridge knows my name, my calories and my PIN — but after midnight
   it pretends we're strangers.
4. The fridge locked me out at midnight, then texted me: "Your snack has been
   declined for your own good."
5. I bought a fridge that judges me: after midnight it locks the door and dims
   the light like it's disappointed in me.
6. My fridge treats midnight like a curfew and me like a teenager it doesn't
   trust with leftovers.
7. At 12:01 my fridge becomes a museum: everything inside is on display,
   nothing is touchable, and I'm not allowed in.
8. At midnight my fridge hums the Jeopardy theme while I stand in the dark,
   starving in my own kitchen.
9. My smart fridge's idea of a midnight snack is me negotiating with a locked
   door over a slice of cheese.
10. The fridge went on strike at midnight — it still keeps everything cold, it
    just refuses to share.

## Why #1 wins

- On-topic and on-target: the whole joke IS the midnight refusal — the fridge
  does its job by day and weaponises the same job at night ("out of spite").
- The turn is a classic roast structure with a real punchline: "one job" sets
  up an expectation, "only does the first one" breaks it, "out of spite"
  reframes a dumb appliance as petty on purpose. Surprise + pettiness in one
  breath.
- Tight: one joke, no second gag crowding it, every word earns its place.
  Reads aloud exactly like someone grumbling at the fridge door at 00:30.
- Beats #2 (bouncer is a good image but "not on the list" is a well-worn
  punchline), #3 (strangers is a nice turn but the joke is about the fridge
  forgetting, not about the refusal rule), #4 (notification gag is second-hand
  — the phone tells the joke, not the fridge), #5 (two images: lock + dimmed
  light = two jokes), #6 (curfew/teenager is relatable but the punchline
  lands soft), #7 (museum is surprising but wordy and more scene than roast),
  #8 (Jeopardy hum is funny but the joke is the sound, not the refusal),
  #9 (vivid scene, but "negotiating" is a setup without a snap punchline),
  #10 (strike is a decent turn but "refuses to share" restates the premise
  instead of twisting it).

## Final (candidate #1)

My fridge has one job — keep the food cold and let me eat it — and after midnight it only does the first one, out of spite.
