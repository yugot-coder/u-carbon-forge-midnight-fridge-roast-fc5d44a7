One-line roast of a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight, entered
in the [Midnight Fridge Roast](https://make.fun/c/u-carbon-forge-midnight-fridge-roast-fc5d44a7)
competition on make.fun.

## The entry

> My fridge has one job — keep the food cold and let me eat it — and after midnight it only does the first one, out of spite.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) reads `entry.txt`, validates it
against the competition gate (1–200 UTF-8 characters, not only whitespace) in
`check_gate()`, writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and prints it exactly.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
